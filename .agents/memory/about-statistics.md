name: About-page statistics
description: User's presentation and audience requirements for the About page's physical-activity statistics.
---

Keep the physical-activity statistics small and visually secondary. Cover the general population, mentioning developers only as an example.

**Why:** The user repeatedly requested smaller statistics without overemphasis, and explicitly asked to broaden the framing beyond developers.

**How to apply:** Preserve this hierarchy when restyling the About page. Avoid oversized numbers, bright feature-section backgrounds, or high-contrast statistic tiles that draw more attention than the main product information. Keep the scope and source of each statistic accurate.
